About me

I am currently a Researcher on the Intelligent Computational Imaging Team at Midea Group's Central Research Center in Shanghai. My work sits at the intersection of AI and healthcare, with a long-term goal of building medical agents that support the whole life-cycle of disease prevention and treatment — from early risk screening and diagnostic assistance through treatment planning and recovery. At Midea I focus on brain-MRI/Alzheimer's diagnostic AI, privacy-preserving federated learning across simulated hospital sites, and LLM-driven autonomous agents for CT/DR examination workflows.

Before joining Midea, I was a Research Fellow at the Lovelace Biomedical Research Institute / Mind Research Network (2023–2025), applying deep learning to neuroimaging studies of traumatic brain injury, sex classification, and cognition. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical & Computer Engineering from Purdue University (2019–2023), advised by Prof. Qingxue Zhang, with a dissertation on wearable big-data harnessing using deep learning and edge computing. Prior to that, I earned my M.S. in Computer Science from The University of Texas at Dallas and my B.Eng. in Telecommunication Engineering from BISTU in Beijing.
